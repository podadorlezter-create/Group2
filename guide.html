Travel Guide

Plan Your Trip

Everything you need to know before visiting the Philippines — from budgeting to local etiquette.

✈️

Getting Around

Domestic flights, ferries, buses, and jeepneys connect the islands. Book flights early for cheaper fares.

☀️

Best Time to Visit

Dry season is from November to May. Avoid the rainy season (June–October) if you plan to island-hop.

💰

Budget & Money

Bring cash for local markets and remote islands. ATMs are available in major cities but not on small islands.

🏨

Where to Stay

Options range from budget hostels (₱500/night) to luxury resorts. Book ahead during peak season.

🎒

Packing Essentials

Reef-safe sunscreen, dry bag, flip-flops, and light clothing. Don't forget a power bank for long trips.

🤝

Local Etiquette

Always say "po" and "opo" to show respect. Remove shoes when entering someone's home.
